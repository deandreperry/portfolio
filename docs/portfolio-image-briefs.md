## Optional photography briefs
Photography is not required to understand these case studies and no stock stand-ins have been inserted. All proposed images are context images, not documentation of research sessions.

### UXD Systems — system-review-context
- Purpose: establish the scale of comparing a dense interface in a design workflow.
- Placement: optional transition after visual-system analysis, never the main evidence image.
- Composition/subject: one monitor displaying the actual comparison screen; small printed token notes with no invented historical annotations.
- Background: quiet neutral desk and wall; no decorative dashboard props.
- Lighting: soft side daylight, controlled screen reflections.
- Camera angle: slight three-quarter eye-level view; screen remains readable.
- Color direction: warm off-white, charcoal, restrained blue.
- Aspect ratio: 3:2 desktop; central 4:5 mobile crop.
- Mobile crop: keep the screen and one contextual object; no critical text at edges.
- Do not include: fake teams, fabricated workshop artifacts, client logos, implied usability sessions.

- Background: neutral working surface.
- Lighting: broad soft daylight, no dramatic neon.
- Camera angle: overhead-oblique with the display legible.
- Color direction: slate, pale green, paper white.
- Aspect ratio: 3:2; mobile 4:5 centered on planner.
- Mobile crop: preserve the proposed-study label.

### 508 Dev — keyboard-context

- Background: simple workstation.
- Lighting: balanced screen and ambient light.

### Palette Snap — color-material-context
- Purpose: connect tactile visual inspiration with a palette exploration.
- Placement: optional near image-to-palette analysis.
- Composition/subject: photographed paper/fabric materials next to the actual Palette Snap result derived from that photograph.
- Background: matte off-white surface.
- Lighting: diffuse color-neutral daylight, consistent white balance.
- Camera angle: overhead, no perspective distortion on materials.
- Color direction: mustard, burgundy, warm neutrals tied to the source photo.
- Aspect ratio: 3:2; mobile crop 1:1 containing both image context and palette.
- Mobile crop: preserve source-to-result relationship.
- Do not include: unrelated gradients, invented export results, claims that harmonious colors automatically pass accessibility checks.
